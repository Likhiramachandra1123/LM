scene 01 / bengaluru, 2018

One morning.
Then you.

I took the bus to college. You were already there, laughing with your friends. I didn’t know your name yet. That ordinary morning was about to change everything.

scene 02 / sapthagiri college of engineering

Somewhere between
hello & home.

Between shared notes and the walk after class, I began saving the little things to tell you. Ordinary days felt different with you in them.

scene 03 / the hours we made ours

A little longer,
just us.

One more cup of chai. Your hand finding mine. From our little roadside dates to a morning above the clouds, I kept wishing the way home were longer.

scene 04 / a quieter kind of love

In the quiet,
always you.

Beside you, with folded hands and the lamps glowing, my wish was simple: whatever comes next, may we keep choosing each other.

scene 05 / pondicherry · 2024 → 2025

Four of us.
A brand-new year.

Breakfast at nine, colourful French-quarter streets, and photos by the sea. By midnight, we were laughing above the city at Chitra’s Cuisine—welcoming 2025 together.

scene 06 / departures & arrivals

An ocean apart.
Still us.

A first goodbye. A return after one year and five months of silence. A small “Sorry!” on the road. Two months later, another departure— and one year after that, the reunion we had been waiting for.

scene 07 / back together, beyond the city

Every road,
closer to you.

After one year apart, we followed winding roads into misty mornings, singing the songs we knew by heart. This time, you were right beside me. No screen between us.

one last page / a new beginning

My Loff.
My every tomorrow.

One little word, only ours. A whole lifetime to mean it.

A college corridor. The long way home. A year of “I miss you.” Now, a lifetime of “I’m right here.” Our favourite chapter is still unwritten. Come be part of it.
